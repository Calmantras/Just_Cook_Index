Main dish

Pasta al Limone

Ingredients

  • 250 g spaghetti
  • 1 organic lemon
  • 60 g Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 bunch basil

Instructions

  1. 1Cook the pasta in plenty of salted water until al dente. Reserve some pasta water.
  2. 2Mix the lemon zest, juice, Parmesan, and olive oil into a creamy sauce.
  3. 3Toss the pasta with the sauce, season to taste, and serve with basil.

Any questions?

The short version.

Everything you need to know about the current beta and planned pricing.

How much does Just Cook! cost during beta?

Invited users can try every available feature for free during beta. No payment details are required.

What is included in the planned Free plan?

The Free plan is intended to include every product feature, with a limit of eight recipe scans in total.

What does fair use mean for recipe scans?

Recipe scans incur API costs. On Monthly, €0.50 in API costs covers about 200 scans; on Lifetime, €2.00 covers about 800. Additional API usage will be available when needed.

Is the Lifetime plan final?

No. The exact definition and terms of Lifetime access will be published transparently and reviewed legally before launch.

Can I share my recipes?

Yes. You can send a recipe link that opens a clean, easy-to-read recipe view for anyone.
